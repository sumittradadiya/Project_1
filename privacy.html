Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Ember Lane Coffee House ("Ember Lane", "we", "us") collects when you visit this website or get in touch with us, and how we use it. It does not cover information collected in person at our shop, such as through payment terminals or loyalty cards, which is handled under separate terms available at the counter.

Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us directly — your name, email address, and message contents when you use the contact form on this site, or when you email or call us.
  • Information collected automatically — basic technical data such as your browser type, device type, and pages visited, collected through standard web server logs.
  • Cookies — this site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Any cookies used are limited to essential site functionality.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to messages sent through the contact form or by email
  • Handle wholesale, catering, or event enquiries
  • Understand how visitors use this website, so we can improve it
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations where applicable

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Sharing your information

We do not share your personal information except:

  • With service providers who help us run this website (such as hosting or email delivery), under obligations to keep it confidential
  • Where required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process

How long we keep information

We keep contact form submissions and related correspondence only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterward for our records, after which it is deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. To do so, email us at hello@emberlane.coffee and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, reach out at hello@emberlane.coffee or visit us at 14 Kadamba Lane, Athwa, Surat, Gujarat 395007.
