Our story

Started with one kettle and a stubborn idea.

Ember Lane began as a single pour-over stand at a Sunday market, run by two former engineers who couldn't find coffee in Surat that tasted like the beans it was made from. Ten years later, we still roast every lot ourselves, in batches small enough to taste the difference between a Wednesday and a Friday.

The counter is teak, reclaimed from a house two streets over. The cups don't match. The coffee always does.
