Frequently asked

Questions we hear at the counter

Visiting

Do you take reservations?

No — we're a walk-in counter with seating for about twenty. Weekday mornings and weekend afternoons are busiest; weekday afternoons are usually quiet.

Is there parking nearby?

There's two-wheeler parking right outside, and a small car park a minute's walk away on Kadamba Lane. Street parking is available but limited on weekends.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, on the outdoor seating area. We keep a water bowl by the door.

Do you have wifi and plugs for working?

We do, though we ask that laptops stay at the four counter-side tables so the rest of the room stays free for people catching up over coffee.

Ordering

What milk alternatives do you offer?

Oat and almond, at no extra charge. Both steam well in our espresso drinks.

How do you choose which single-origin is on filter?

We rotate weekly based on what's tasting best in our morning cupping. Ask the counter which lot is pouring — we're always glad to talk through it.

Can I get a menu item to go?

Everything on the menu is available to go, including pour-overs. We use compostable cups for takeaway coffee.

Do you cater for allergies?

Our kitchen handles nuts and gluten, so we can't guarantee an allergen-free item, but the counter can talk you through ingredients for anything on the menu.

Beans & roasting

Do you sell bags of beans to take home?

Yes — 250g bags of whatever's currently roasting are sold at the counter, and we'll grind to your brew method on request.

Can I visit the roastery?

The roaster sits behind the counter, so you can watch it from your table any day we're open. We don't currently run separate roastery tours.

Do you wholesale to other cafes or offices?

We do, on a limited basis. Get in touch through the contact page and we'll follow up about volume and pricing.

Still have a question?

We answer these ourselves — no chatbot on the other end.
